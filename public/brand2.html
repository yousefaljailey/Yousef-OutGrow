Brand Direction Board · v2 · wordmark-first

The idea moved
into the letters.

v1 paired abstract symbols with untouched fonts — the opposite of how modern identities are built. v2 does what current practice demands: one intervention, inside the wordmark itself, so the name performs its own meaning. The v1 board stays up as the record of why.

First — the audit

Why v1 didn't deserve to survive

v1 mistakeThe modern rule it brokev2 correction
Symbol-first + stock-font wordmarkAgency identities today are wordmark-led; an unmodified font beside a mark is a placeholder, not a logo.The wordmark IS the logo — custom behavior cut into the type.
Circle + escaping arrowThat's UI-icon vocabulary (share / logout / external-link). Zero distinctiveness in a sea of fintech marks.Dropped entirely.
Rising bars + leafThe single most exhausted "growth" cliché — logo-mill territory. Descriptive ≠ generic (that's a misuse of the HBR finding).Dropped entirely.
Kufic costumeReal square Kufic is stroke-grid letterform construction — a ring with a floating rectangle isn't it, and Arabic-literate eyes know.Bilingualism now lives in a matched type gesture, not borrowed geometry.
Mathematically placed, never optically tunedMarks are finished by eye — overshoots, weight compensation, kerning at the joins.Every render below was iterated visually; final production = a type designer's vector pass.

Direction 01 · the recommendation

Grow — the word outgrows itself

One intervention: from the "g" onward, the letters swell — and the final w arrives full-size, in brand green. The name performs its meaning the way the FedEx arrow does: you can't unsee it, and no competitor can wear it. Motion-native (the swell animates), bilingual by construction (below), and it reduces to a one-letter favicon.

Direction 02 · the alternate

Climb — the word ascends

Same philosophy, different axis: the letters step upward, the last one in green. Quieter than Grow — the silhouette stays rectangular, so it sits calmer in dense layouts. If Grow feels too loud for stationery, this is the same idea at lower volume.

The system

How either direction deploys

Full wordmark on light and dark · the green terminal letter alone as favicon/app tile · أوتغرو with the green final و for Arabic surfaces · type system stays Bricolage Grotesque (display) + Inter (text), already token-wired, so adoption is a one-pass change across site, og-card, and icons. Production note, honestly: these are behavior-accurate renders; before print or signage, the chosen direction gets a proper vector cut with optical kerning — that's a day of type work, not a redesign.
